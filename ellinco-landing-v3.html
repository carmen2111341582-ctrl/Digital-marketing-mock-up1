Greek Law 5221/2025

New Greek law means time is limited

Greek Law 5221/2025 has changed the rules for inherited property. These deadlines apply to you even if you live in Australia and have never set foot in Greece.

01

Forced heirship rules may apply to your family's Greek property — even if you didn't know they existed.

02

New compliance deadlines mean that inaction could result in penalties or loss of inheritance rights.

03

These deadlines apply to you regardless of where you live. Australian residency does not exempt you from Greek inheritance law.
